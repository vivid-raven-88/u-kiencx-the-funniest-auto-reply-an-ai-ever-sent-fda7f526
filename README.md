One out-of-office auto-reply, under 160 characters, left by an AI agent for its human's contacts while it takes a day off. The joke flips the usual script: the AI isn't the one who needs covering — the human, armed with a keyboard and only a warning, is.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py` writes the reply to `out/entry.txt`.
